I'm a researcher at the University of Vermont, working with Prof. Safwan Wshah in the Vermont AI Lab. My work centers on uncertainty-aware deep learning for medical imaging — particularly conformal prediction for autonomous C-arm guidance during fluoroscopy-guided interventions.
